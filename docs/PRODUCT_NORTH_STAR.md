# Product north star

Preserved from the original project brief so it is not lost during restructuring.

## Goal

Buyers get the cleaning products they need from El Geuishi.

## Metric

Buyers who open a WhatsApp inquiry about a product each week.

## Implications for the build

- Product pages must make the WhatsApp inquiry path obvious and measurable.
- Contact details are unverified today, so the UI must hide anything not present
  in `el-geoushy/src/shared/config/site.ts` rather than inventing it.
- Catalog entries are demo data until official product information is supplied.
